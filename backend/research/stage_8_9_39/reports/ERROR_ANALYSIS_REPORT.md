## 1. Overview & Methodology

A granular failure analysis was conducted across all predictions generated by Configuration B ($\theta = 0.24$) and Configuration D ($\theta = 0.34$) across both evaluation cohorts (`v4_historical_test`, $N=293$ and `v4_reserved_expansion_test`, $N=102$).

To preserve scientific rigor:
1. Predictions are analyzed per acoustic category and source group to identify structural biases.
2. Correlated clips originating from the same source group or author are treated as clustered observations, not independent errors.
3. No automated relabeling was performed. A dedicated human-review queue of 44 ambiguous recordings was generated in `backend/research/stage_8_9_39/results/metrics/human_review_queue.csv`.

| Unmasked Scream FNs | 7 | 11 | 5 shared clips |

### Core Acoustic Root Causes:
1. **Harmonic Phonation Overlap in Sports Chants:** Chanting groups create quasi-periodic harmonic structures between $800\text{ Hz}$ and $2000\text{ Hz}$ that trigger both YAMNet speech-screaming classes and acoustic phonation energy heads.
2. **Whistle Resonances:** Referee whistles concentrate intense sinusoidal energy at $2.5\text{--}3.5\text{ kHz}$. While subband filters attempted to penalize narrowband whistle harmonics, highly reverberant whistles in festival environments spread energy across adjacent bands, evading rejection.
3. **Infant Crying Acoustic Congruence:** Infant cries share the high fundamental frequency, non-linear vocal bifurcations, and roughness of human distress screams, representing a fundamental biophysical acoustic overlap.
