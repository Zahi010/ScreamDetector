### 2. Tier 2 Decision Mechanics & Reason Codes

The Stage 8.9.22 verifier (`src/sddfr_classifier.py`) evaluates candidate detections from Tier 1:

```
                            Tier 1 Probability (p1)
                                       │
                      ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
                      ▼                                 ▼
                 p1 < 0.3500                       p1 >= 0.3500
              [TIER1_REJECT]                    [TRIGGER TIER 2]
                                                        │
                                                        ▼
                                           Multi-Feature Verification
                                         (w_HNR, w_SHR, w_pitch, etc.)
                                                        │
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┐
                      ▼                                 ▼                                 ▼
             POSSIBLE_SCREAM                     LIKELY_CONFUSER                      UNCERTAIN
         (p2 >= 0.40 OR Chaos)              (p2 < 0.20 & Multi-Valid)            (Ambiguous / Low SNR)
                      │                                 │                                 │
                      ▼                                 ▼                                 ▼
             FINAL DECISION = 1                FINAL DECISION = 0                FINAL DECISION = 1
         [TIER2_CONFIRMED_SCREAM]          [TIER2_SUPPRESSED_CONFUSER]        [UNCERTAIN_PRESERVE_SAFETY]
```

#### Safe Suppression Gating Rules:
An alert generated by Tier 1 can **ONLY** be suppressed to `0` if:
1. **SNR is sufficient:** $\text{estimated\_snr} \ge 6.0\text{ dB}$ (in Low SNR, modal features are untrustworthy).
2. **Multiple reliable modal features agree:**
   - Harmonic regularity: $w_{\text{HNR}} > 0.40$ AND $\text{HNR} > 12.0\text{ dB}$.
   - Vocal fold periodicity: $w_{\text{pitch}} > 0.40$ AND $\text{high\_energy\_pitch\_failure} < 0.15$.
   - Subharmonic absence: $w_{\text{SHR}} > 0.40$ AND $\text{SHR} < -25.0\text{ dB}$.
3. **If features are noisy or ambiguous:** The decision falls back to `UNCERTAIN_PRESERVE_SAFETY` (alert preserved).
