## 1. Executive Summary & Shortfall Analysis

While Stage 76 successfully established an authenticated, zero-leakage, CC0/CC BY pipeline and acquired 26 provenance-verified recordings across 18 source groups, a significant shortfall remains relative to the Stage 75 planning target of 1,750 clips.

### Root Causes of Shortfall:
1. **Strict Licensing Filtering:** 61.2% (41 of 67) of discovered candidate clips were quarantined due to Non-Commercial (CC BY-NC) or Copyleft (CC BY-SA) restrictions.
2. **Historical Uploader Blacklist:** All clips uploaded by the 612 historical creators were strictly barred, eliminating high-volume uploaders.
3. **Single Query Saturation:** Standard keyword queries yielded limited public-domain clips under strict duration constraints.
4. **Storage Quota Adherence:** Acquisition was paced to respect the 500 MB C: drive quota ceiling.

## 3. Recommended Remediation for Future Acquisition Cycles

1. Expand search queries to include multi-lingual distress vocalizations (e.g., 'shriek', 'crying infant', 'bambino pianto', 'hurlement').
2. Engage acoustic research partnerships for direct field recordings of outdoor crowd ambience.
3. Prioritize completing human double-blind annotation on Batch 1 (26 recordings) before initiating Batch 2 acquisition.
