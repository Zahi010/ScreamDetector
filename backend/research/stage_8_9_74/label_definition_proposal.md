## 1. Rationale & Purpose

The binary classification paradigm (`scream` vs `non-scream`) is fundamentally inadequate for modeling real-world acoustic distress in public spaces. In complex acoustic environments like music festivals, protests, and sporting events, human vocalizations exist along continuous dimensions of:
- **Acoustic Effort & Loudness:** Whispering $\to$ Conversational $\to$ Projected $\to$ Maximal Shriek.
- **Vocal Fold Chaos:** Laminar periodic phonation $\to$ Subharmonic bifurcation $\to$ Full turbulent chaos.
- **Acoustic Channel Conditions:** Clean near-field $\to$ Crowd-masked $\to$ Reverberant hall $\to$ Low SNR distant.

This document proposes a standardized 11-category acoustic taxonomy to replace forced binary annotations in future prospective acquisitions.

## 2. Standardized Vocalization & Acoustic Categories

### Category 1: Adult Human Scream (`VOCAL_SCREAM_ADULT`)
- **Definition:** Maximal-effort vocalization produced by an adult or adolescent human under acute fear, pain, panic, or distress.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** High fundamental frequency ($F_0 \in [350, 1000]$ Hz), severe vocal fold desynchronization producing auditory roughness in the 30–150 Hz modulation band, high high-frequency energy ratio ($HF > 0.10$), and rapid spectral flux ($flux > 0.040$).
- **Inclusion Criteria:** Authentic distress screams, horror shrieks, high-intensity pain vocalizations.
- **Exclusion Criteria:** Theatrical staged voice acting without physiological distress, shouted words with recognizable lexical formants.

### Category 2: Child Scream (`VOCAL_SCREAM_CHILD`)
- **Definition:** High-pitch maximal-effort scream produced by a prepubescent child ($< 12$ years).
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Extremely high fundamental frequency ($F_0 \in [500, 2000]$ Hz), lower auditory roughness ($roughness \in [0.010, 0.030]$) due to smaller vocal fold mass and higher fundamental pitch, and concentrated lower-formant energy ($1$–$3$ kHz).
- **Inclusion Criteria:** Play scream, startle shriek, pain scream from young children.
- **Boundary Note:** Must NOT be vetoed by adult auditory roughness rules.

### Category 3: Infant Crying & Colic Distress (`VOCAL_INFANT_CRY`)
- **Definition:** Rhythmic or sustained acoustic distress vocalization from an infant ($< 2$ years).
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Periodic cry-bursts punctuated by rapid inhalations; acoustic chaos and high roughness ($0.025$–$0.060$) present during peak distress, but exhibiting rhythmic temporal modulation ($0.5$–$2.0$ Hz bursts).
- **Exclusion Criteria:** Child screams, adult weeping.

### Category 4: Adult Crying & Sobbing (`VOCAL_ADULT_CRY`)
- **Definition:** Audible sorrow or grief vocalization featuring spasmodic respiratory gasps, tremulous pitch contour, and low-to-moderate acoustic intensity.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Low roughness, low spectral flux, periodic pitch instability without high-frequency scream energy.

### Category 5: Shouted Speech (`VOCAL_SHOUTED_SPEECH`)
- **Definition:** High-effort projected vocalization containing distinct linguistic phonemes, consonant closures, and vowel formant transitions.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Clear vowel formants ($F_1, F_2, F_3$), fundamental frequency generally below $400$ Hz, moderate roughness, and elevated spectral flux driven by consonant transitions ($flux > 0.150$).
- **Inclusion Criteria:** Aggressive yelling, stadium chants, vocal commands ("HEY!", "STOP!").

### Category 6: Excited Cheering & Celebratory Outcries (`VOCAL_CHEER_POSITIVE`)
- **Definition:** High-arousal positive-affect vocal burst produced during celebration, sports victories, or festival performance drops.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Rapid pitch glides, wide harmonic spread, intermittent cheering, and absence of sustained vocal fold chaos.

### Category 7: Collective Crowd Vocalization (`VOCAL_CROWD_BABBLE`)
- **Definition:** Acoustic sum of tens to thousands of simultaneous talkers, laughers, and chanters.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Diffuse temporal envelope, stationary noise-like spectrum, absence of single-source dominance.

### Category 8: Narrowband Whistle (`SFX_WHISTLE_TONAL`)
- **Definition:** Resonant, non-vocal pure or harmonic tone generated by athletic whistles, referee pipes, or high-pitch metal squeaks.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Extreme high-frequency concentration ($HF > 10.0$), high harmonic-to-noise ratio ($HNR > 12.0$ dB), and minimal spectral flatness ($flatness < 0.001$).

### Category 9: Sirens & Frequency-Modulated SFX (`SFX_SIREN_SWEEP`)
- **Definition:** Mechanical or electronic emergency vehicle sirens, fireworks, or synth risers.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Continuous sinusoidal or triangular frequency modulation across $500$–$2500$ Hz; high cross-window correlation without chaotic phonation.

### Category 10: Environmental Transients & Machinery (`ENV_TRANSIENT`)
- **Definition:** Broadband friction, percussive transients, clapping, doors slamming, metal clatter, or engine noise.
- **Acoustic Signatures:** Aperiodic energy, wide spectral distribution without harmonic combs.

### Category 11: Ambiguous / Indeterminate Vocalization (`VOCAL_AMBIGUOUS`)
- **Definition:** Any vocal burst where blinded expert raters cannot conclusively separate scream, shout, gasp, or laughter due to noise masking or boundary acoustics.
- **Inclusion Criteria:** Surprise "oooh" gasps, stifled throat clearing, low-SNR masked events (< -6 dB).
- **Protocol Action:** Quarantined from binary benchmark evaluation cohorts.

## 3. Implementation Protocol for Future Benchmarks

1. **Pre-Registration:** Any future prospective dataset must be labeled using this 11-class schema before model scoring.
2. **Double-Blind Review:** A minimum of two independent reviewers must assign primary and secondary tags.
3. **Targeted Gate Evaluation:** Binary metrics (Gate 1 through Gate 4) will be computed by mapping Categories 1 and 2 to Positives, Categories 5 through 10 to Negatives, and Category 11 to an Isolated Diagnostic Queue.
