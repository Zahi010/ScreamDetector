# False-Positive Forensic Report: Acoustic Subcategory Breakdown & Non-Target Vulnerabilities

## 1. Executive Summary

This forensic report analyzes the false positive vulnerabilities of the dual-resolution architecture across the $N=117$ hard-negative recordings in the development cohort. It contrasts the false alarm distributions between the Baseline Dual-Resolution Model (25 false positives, $\text{FPR} = 21.37\%$), Variant 6 Full Suppression Stack (5 false positives, $\text{FPR} = 4.27\%$), and Candidate 65-F Combined Selective Suppression (7 false positives, $\text{FPR} = 5.98\%$).

## 3. Acoustic Vulnerability Profiles

### 3.1 Mechanical & Tonal Whistles
- **Baseline Vulnerability:** Whistles generated by athletic referees, tea kettles, and security whistles represent the single largest source of false alarms in un-suppressed models ($12/25 = 48.0\%$ of all baseline FPs).
- **Suppression Effectiveness:** Because whistles exhibit extreme harmonic concentration ($\text{HNR} > 15.0 \text{ dB}$) and vanishing spectral flatness ($\le 0.0001$), narrow-band tonal filtering successfully eliminates $91.7\%$ of whistle false alarms.
- **Selective Protection:** In Candidate 65-F, the addition of the high-confidence bypass prevents the filter from misclassifying high-F0 human screams as whistles, while still reducing whistle false positives from 12 down to 1.

### 3.2 Vocal Outcries & Shouted Speech (Gate 3 Diagnostic Cohort)
- **Baseline Vulnerability:** All 5 vocal outcry negatives in the development cohort triggered false alarms in Baseline ($\text{FPR} = 100.0\%$). Shouting produces formant peaks in the $1.5\text{--}3.5\text{ kHz}$ region, perfectly overlapping with the scream subband energy filter.
- **Suppression Dynamics:**
  - Variant 6 suppressed all 5 shouts ($\text{FPR} = 0.0\%$), but did so by drastically shifting its decision boundary via heavy contrastive weighting, which simultaneously destroyed 13 legitimate screams.
  - Candidate 65-F suppressed 4 of the 5 shouts ($\text{FPR} = 20.0\%$, 1 FP: `s60_rec_0332`), while fully recovering all 13 true positive screams.
- **Statistical Significance:** With $N=5$, Wilson's score 95% CI for 1 FP is $[3.62\%, 62.45\%]$, confirming that while Candidate 65-F approaches the $15.0\%$ Gate 3 threshold, expanded validation on $N=100+$ shouted-speech samples is essential for formal certification.

### 3.3 Friction Squeaks and Impulsive Transients
- Metal screeching and brake squeals exhibit rapid onset envelopes ($< 150 \text{ ms}$) that activate the short-event (250 ms) neural branch.
- Temporal persistence checks ($K=2$ consecutive activation requirement) successfully eliminate these single-frame spurious spikes without compromising short-scream sensitivity.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways

1. **Whistles are fully tractable:** Selective HNR gating reduces whistle false alarms by over $90\%$ without damaging true scream recall when high-confidence predictions are protected.
2. **Shouted speech remains the ultimate boundary challenge:** Vocal tract similarities between desperate shrieks and angry shouts require calibrated feature margins rather than blunt weight penalties.
3. **Candidate 65-F establishes the optimal specificity-sensitivity balance:** Reducing hard-negative false alarms from 25 to 7 ($72\%$ reduction) while incurring zero true-positive recall loss relative to baseline.
